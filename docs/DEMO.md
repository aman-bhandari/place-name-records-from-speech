# Demo script — Eknaam (P-015), about three minutes

Start: `./run.sh serve` → http://127.0.0.1:8015

1. **The problem in one screen (20 s).** Open the queue. Point at the counts: names, recordings, distinct speakers, all
   pending. "Every name here was said by several real people in All India Radio bulletins and in AI4Bharat's
   IndicVoices; the system cut the name out of each sentence."
2. **A clean case (40 s).** Open देहरादून. The sheet entry: Devanagari, DEHRADUN, /d̪eːɦraːd̪uːn/. Click two waveforms,
   then "in sentence" on one. Show the witness columns: two Hindi recognisers and the phones heard. Show the confidence
   parts: clips agree, spelling explains clips, lead over the next spelling, speakers.
3. **Reconciled, not picked (40 s).** Open a medium case with disagreement (e.g. करौली or उज्जैन). Competing spellings
   with support bars and supporter counts; the recommended one is the spelling that best explains all clips, not the
   clearest clip. A regional variant kept as a linked alternate where two or more speakers say it differently.
4. **Roman spelling with a reason (30 s).** Open मसूरी or पिथौरागढ़: scheme gives Masuri / Pithauragarh; the established
   form is kept and the reason stated. Open Method → the Hunterian table and the exception list.
5. **Officer decision and audit (40 s).** Edit the Devanagari in the decision panel: the IPA and scheme Roman re-derive
   live. Approve with your name. The audit trail gains a hashed entry; Audit trail page shows the chain verifies.
   Export approved → CSV.
6. **Field recordings (20 s).** Add recordings: upload three phone clips of one name; it is reconciled and enters the queue.
7. **Results (10 s).** Results page numbers: consensus vs single clip vs oracle, noise sweep, calibration by band.
